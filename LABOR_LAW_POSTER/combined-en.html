E-Verify Participation Notice

What is E-Verify?

E-Verify is a free online system run by the U.S. Department of Homeland Security and the Social Security Administration. It compares the information from an employee’s Form I-9 with government records to confirm that the employee is authorized to work in the United States.

Employers may use it only after a job offer is accepted and the Form I-9 is completed, never to screen applicants. If a worker cannot be confirmed, the employer must tell them and let them resolve it before taking any action.

Who must post this notice

Employers enrolled in E-Verify must post the E-Verify Participation poster, in English and Spanish, next to this poster. In Florida, private employers with 25 or more employees must use E-Verify (Fla. Stat. §448.095).

Why it is not printed here

The Department of Homeland Security provides the official poster only through each employer’s own E-Verify account and does not permit its commercial reproduction. Download it from your account at e-verify.gov.
